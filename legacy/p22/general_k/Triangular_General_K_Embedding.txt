P22 — Exact acquisition on narrow diagonal bands and a general-parameter bracket
23 October 2026. New derivation in this working cycle; historical priority unconfirmed.
All K>=3,N>=2. Full independent band row domain T_d={0<=f<=c<=1,c-f<=d}, with 0<d<=1, unit prices, positive-input certificate benchmark, zero-error all-input correctness. This note uses the independently reviewed ordinal/Ramsey theorem of P21 Appendix A; it does not replace that theorem with a finite computation.

THEOREM G1 (band general-parameter bracket and an exact wide-parameter regime).
Put D=K+3N-3, B=ceil(dN)-1, D_d=D-B=K+floor((3-d)N)-2 and let delta_K be P21 Theorem1's balanced inversion overhead. Then
  1+delta_K(N-1)/D <= R_rand,d(K,N)
                       <= 1+[delta_K(N-1)+B]/(D-B),
  KN/D <= R_det,d(K,N) <= KN/(D-B).
In particular, whenever 0<dN<=1, the optima are EXACT for every K>=3,N>=1:
  R_rand,d(K,N)=1+delta_K(N-1)/D,
  R_det,d(K,N)=KN/D.
These are the same numerical optima as the unbounded-cost independent domain. They follow from an exact certificate bridge and a legal bounded embedding, not from comparator equality by a heuristic domain limit.
For each fixed N and each fixed d>0, as K tends to infinity, the randomised optimum tends to (2N+1)/3 and the deterministic optimum tends to N. For N=1 both equal1. Outside dN<=1 the finite bounds need not be tight. For K=3 the randomised lower here is only1 and the other new P22 three-group results are more informative. The excluded d=0 domain has no GOOD archive: all means are diagonal and either dominated or supported, so no positive-class ratio is assigned there.

PROOF OF THE NEW LOWER EMBEDDING.
Let psi(z)=6/5+z/[5(z+1)] for positive rational z, so 6/5<psi<7/5 and psi is strictly increasing. Set c=2/3,f=1/6, l(u)=(cu+f)/(1+u), r(v)=c+f/v. Candidate rows are all (c/N,f/N). For each labelled L threshold u=psi(z_L), use first row (1/3,1/3), N-2 middle zero rows and last row (l(u)-1/3,l(u)-1/3). For each R threshold v=psi(z_R), use first row (3/4,0), N-2 zero middle rows and last row (r(v)-3/4,0).
First construct the following base rows in T_1, then multiply EVERY actual row in all groups by d to obtain the final archive. All final rows belong to T_d: diagonal rows have gap zero, right rows have gap at most3d/4 and the candidate gap is d/(2N). The two endpoints(0,0),(1,1) remain legal completion rows even though they need not occur in the actual embedding. For the base construction, 29/66<l(u)<11/24 and 11/14<r(v)<29/36; in particular 1/3<l<c and 3/4<r<1. Actual final means are p=d(c,f)/N,L=d(l,l)/N,R=d(r,0)/N. The left supporting threshold (l-f)/(c-l) is u and the right threshold f/(r-c) is v. Consequently the two points make p strictly unsupported exactly when u<v. All full rivals are Pareto-safe.
Every positive certificate includes the whole candidate: if one candidate row is unread, complete it at (1,1), leave the remaining rows actual, and use an actual R mean. The candidate completion has cost at least1/N and failure at least1/N, while R has cost dr/N<1/N and failure0, so R dominates it. Every rival requires its first row, and that row is P2-safe against the complete candidate: left failure d/(3N)>d/(6N); right cost3d/(4N)>2d/(3N).
After candidate census, every incomplete rival's upper corner has each coordinate at least1/N, hence is strictly northeast of p. It supplies no supporting-weight restriction. Therefore TRUE needs two complete opposite-side witnesses. In each embedded unique-inversion archive the exact comparator is D=N+(K-1)+2(N-1).
All first and intervening rows are fixed independently of hidden thresholds. Only a final row reveals its threshold. At every no-inversion transcript, fill unread rivals with (1,1) and leave the candidate actual; the incomplete rivals are northeast and the completed no-inversion rivals share a supporting weight, so TRUE is not licensed. The original Appendix A compression integrates arbitrary prefix interleavings, with residual cost N-1 per completed label. Its causal finite-action Ramsey reduction and fixed unique-inversion pattern therefore apply without changing the legal query space, the per-input comparator or the order of quantifiers. The randomised lower follows. This remains a supremal lower using arbitrarily large numerical alphabets. When d is rational, the actual rows are rational; for irrational d the scaled rows need not be rational. No uniform finite-precision theorem follows.

DETERMINISTIC LOWER, WITHOUT RAMSEY.
Apply the same overall multiplication by d to this deterministic construction. Keep the same candidate. Use one fixed R anchor with first row (3/4,0) and all remaining rows zero. Each of K-2 L groups has first row(1/3,1/3), zero middle rows and final row either0 (good) or(1/4,1/4) (bad). With anchor v=2, the good L has u=1/2 and bad L has u=5. The all-bad archive is supported, and changing just an unread L final row to0 gives a positive archive with exact comparator D. Simulate any fixed deterministic correct policy on the all-bad archive until the last L final disclosure, assigning that row0 in the final fixed archive. Before that disclosure, every previously completed L is bad. The policy must acquire every L and then, for its positive certificate, the complete candidate and anchor, hence all KN rows. This defines one archive for the deterministic policy before any actual execution; it is not an adversary applied separately to random tapes. The endpoint false completions above prove candidate and witness census necessities. The construction already lies in six symbols including the admissible endpoints; no minimal-alphabet assertion.

UPPER AND LIMIT.
The band contains both whole-vector endpoints, so the square corner test is exact on every realised band archive. By the separate P22 exact band-feasibility theorem, every strict witness triple has total observed length greater than(3-d)N, hence at leastfloor((3-d)N)+1. Every other group requires at leastone observation. Thus C_d*>=D_d. Completing just the candidate and its two selected witnesses adds at most3N-[floor((3-d)N)+1]=ceil(dN)-1=B observations. Existing robust P2 is preserved by revealing additional rows; the known strict mixture remains below the realised candidate. The resulting state is an unbounded-domain TRUE certificate, so
  C_d* <= C_U* <= C_d*+B.
The unbounded acquisition policy costs at most C_U*+delta_K(N-1), establishing the randomised upper. Census costs KN, establishing the deterministic upper. When B=0 the certificate comparators agree on EVERY positive band archive and the two brackets close. In particular, every band TRUE triple in that regime must have all3Nrows, consistently with the unbounded certificate structure. As delta_K/K tends to2/3, the two fixed-N brackets have the stated limits. No simultaneous K,N asymptotic is inferred.

SHARPNESS OF MINIMUM BAND CERTIFICATE.
For three groups take a=j=N and i=floor(N(1-d))+1. Then s=(N-i)/N<d and r=t=0. The exact P22 band-feasibility construction applies. Its candidate lower failure is(d-s)/2-epsilon<1/(2N), since 0<d-s<=1/N. All candidate rows are disclosed, so this is its actual failure. Every additional rival repeats(1,1); its first row gives lower failure1/N greater than the candidate failure and is P2-safe, while the existing pair forces unsupportedness. The total certificate cost is2N+i+(K-3)=D_d. This also covers N=1 withi=1. No unseen additional rival can be left at prefix zero, because its all-zero completion would defeat GOOD.
