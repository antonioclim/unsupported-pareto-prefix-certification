CHANGES IN THE PRESENT ANALYSIS

derive failure=1-reliability, form deterministic partial-sum bounds and create
computational reveal transcripts and geometric certificates. Those outputs
are new secondary analyses/extractions of the cited CC BY 4.0 data. They do not
represent newly measured radio experiments. Retain this attribution, the source
