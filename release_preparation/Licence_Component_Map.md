# Component rights — P46

The P35 grant adopted on 24 October 2026 applies within the author's rights.
The governing notice is `../LICENSING.md`; exact files and archive members are
listed in `../LICENSE_SCOPE.json`.

| Material | Applicable terms | Preservation requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Covered author-owned code and executable configuration | BSD-3-Clause | Retain the applicable copyright notice and standard terms. |
| Covered author-owned documentation, certificate data, tables, result records, metadata and figures | CC BY 4.0 | Retain attribution and the licence link; identify changes as required. |
| Original APEX data, README and secondary extracts | Upstream CC BY 4.0; DOI 10.3217/3dhx0-2k125 | Retain the original attribution and transformation notices. No new ownership of source data is claimed. |
| DejaVu fonts and embedded subsets/glyphs | Original DejaVu/Bitstream/Arev terms where applicable | Retain the bundled font notices. |
| Standard licence texts and original third-party notices | Original status | Preserve the applicable texts. |
| Current manuscript, supplement, cover letter, bibliography and private submission material | Outside the public grant | Existing rights are unchanged. |
| Mixed and historical ZIP containers | Per-member terms | Retain the governing scope, applicable licence texts and source notices with extracted members. |

The associated APEX firmware/framework is not bundled or executed. Its separate
software licence does not govern the dataset or the new code as a whole.

Historical rights records remain unchanged evidence of their original state.
The adopted grant supersedes their pending-licence wording only for covered
author-owned components. It does not change third-party terms or excluded-file
rights. Full standard texts are in `../LICENSES/`.
