S5.4. Priced and dependent extensions; a limit of greedy information utility

Heterogeneous disclosure prices. Different records may have different processing or contractual disclosure fees. Prices fixed publicly by group and position already fit the weighted comparator Cw*C_w^*. The priced band results cover their stated regimes, and legal prefetch has the explicit factor Γ(w)\Gamma(w) in Section S4.2. Cost-aware policies for more general bounded domains remain to be analysed. In particular, neither the unit-price square ratio nor the triangle constants 12/1112/11 and 87/8087/80 are established for arbitrary tariffs. The adverse controls in Section S3.5 show why retaining the original policy while changing its account is insufficient.

Public dependence with a sharp distortion bound. Public relations between records change the legal completion class and hence its realised certificates. General priced-function formulations already permit a non-product input domain (CM08); stochastic certification under correlated distributions addresses a different objective and comparator (GMS26). The following result quantifies a specific dependence structure under the present paid-prefix contract. Independence below means that the legal block completions form a Cartesian product, not that a probabilistic model is assumed.

S5.9. Few-alternative certificates in many criteria

Theorem S12 bounds the number of rivals needed to rule out support. A complementary bound controls the number of criteria needed to establish support. pA is unsupported in ℝd⇔pA is unsupported in every two-coordinate projection. \begin{gathered} p_A\text{ is unsupported in }\mathbb R^d\\ \Longleftrightarrow\quad p_A\text{ is unsupported in every two-coordinate projection}. \end{gathered}

∃λ∈[0,1]λ(pLi−pRi)<pAi−pRi(1≤i≤d). \exists\lambda\in[0,1]\quad \lambda(p_{Li}-p_{Ri})<p_{Ai}-p_{Ri} \quad(1\leq i\leq d).

S5.11. Exact anchor reduction and preservation of paid prefixes

Objective reduction has an established literature on dominance preservation (BZ09); rank-based reductions can also change the ordering cone (LZ26). The following sufficient condition records the additional support and observation requirements needed here.
