t=1/20,g=29/100,s=0,r=29/100,G=g+t, t=1/20,\quad g=29/100,\quad s=0,\quad r=29/100,\quad G=g+t,

which satisfies (C9), yet gives (t+r)/(3−t−s−r)=17/133>1/8(t+r)/(3-t-s-r)=17/133>1/8 in the southeast-first excess envelope. This is a failure of that upper-bound argument, not a counterexample to every possible 9/89/8 policy or a claim that every envelope is attained by its policy. The optimised threshold in main Section 6 includes candidate-partial comparators.
