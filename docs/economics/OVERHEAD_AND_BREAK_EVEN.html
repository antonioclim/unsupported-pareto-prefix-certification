S12.4. Measured local overhead and conditional break-even accounting

The additional APEX timing diagnostic ran each implementation once for warm-up and then nine times on the same 1,536-task cohort, rotating and reversing execution order. CPU process time includes local next-row calls, rational arithmetic and bookkeeping, but excludes archive loading and transport. The measured median CPU times were 0.0876156680.087615668 seconds for full groupwise census, 0.7627515660.762751566 seconds for census with FALSE stopping and 0.9560545900.956054590 seconds for screened deterministic acquisition with FALSE stopping. The respective observed ranges were [0.080700124,0.286775860][0.080700124,0.286775860], [0.680230609,4.018544470][0.680230609,4.018544470] and [0.868180009,1.585645396][0.868180009,1.585645396] seconds. This shared-container diagnostic, including its large outliers, is not a stable hardware or deployed-service benchmark. In particular, fewer purchased records did not imply less local computation.

The difference between the latter two medians is 0.1933030240.193303024 seconds. In an assumed additive sequential model with equal external access latency per record and no batching, overlap or caching, its 850 saved disclosures would offset that difference when access latency exceeds

0.193303024850s≃0.2274ms \frac{0.193303024}{850}\ \mathrm{s} \simeq0.2274\ \mathrm{ms}

per record. This is a conditional calculation from the reported diagnostic, not a measured end-to-end speedup or a portable latency threshold.

For a monetary evaluation, let cc be a common charge per returned record and let HH be the additional total computation, setup and service cost of the wrapped screened implementation relative to wrapped census on this same cohort. Its net saving is

850c−H, 850c-H,

which is positive exactly when H<850cH<850c. Neither cc nor HH was measured. For heterogeneous tariffs, compute each policy’s total from the actual disclosed locations; two different wrapped policies need not have nested transcripts, so the unweighted difference 850 cannot simply be multiplied by an arbitrary average price. Lemma S15’s pathwise comparison applies to a policy and its own wrapper.

More generally, if a proposed workload has positive frequency π\pi, conditional expected reductions d+d_+ and d−d_- against a declared baseline, common disclosure price cc and expected additional overhead hh, the expected net gain is the accounting identity

c[πd++(1−π)d−]−h. c\bigl[\pi d_++(1-\pi)d_-\bigr]-h.
