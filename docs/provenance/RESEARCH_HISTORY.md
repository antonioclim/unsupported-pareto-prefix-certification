### S9.2. Earlier controls retained in their original archive
The earlier research archive is `legacy/p20/output/Research_Companion_Paid_Prefix_v1.zip`. Its internal root is `Exact_Acquisition_Research_Companion_v1/`. The paths below are inside that root, rather than flat paths beside the current runner:

- `Source_Version_Notes_v1.txt` records the earlier source versions and reading limits. It is a historical record, not evidence that every cited publication was read in full.
- `REPLAY_JOBS.json`, `REPLAY_VALIDATION.json` and `README.txt` specify the earlier declared replay and its retained validation record.
- `p14/theory/lower_k3n3_check_v1.py`, `p14/theory/lower_n2_dichotomy_check_v1.py` and `p15/theory_lower/twoworld25_k3n3_check_v1.py` concern the exact square cases in Section S1.
- `p15/analysis/independent_k3n3_execution_attempt_v1.json` preserves the failed initial coverage assertion. `p15/analysis/independent_k3n3_check_v2.py`, its versioned protocol, fixtures and `independent_k3n3_results_v2.json` preserve the corrected coverage check. The failed attempt is not counted as a successful independent confirmation.
- `p16/analysis/independent_finite_check_v1.py`, `p16/precision_lower/dyadic_exact_check_v1.py`, `p16/precision_upper/endpoint_transfer_check_v1.py` and `p17/domain_transfer/joint_domain_check_v1.py` contain the finite-domain and legal-completion controls summarised in Table S13.
- `p17/setup_cost/setup_cost_check_v1.py` contains the exact square setup controls in Section S5.2. The coded-prefix simulator and its versioned protocol are under `p16/applied_codec/`.
- `p17/applied_replay/small_catalogue_replay_v1.py`, `small_catalogue_results_v1.json`, `small_catalogue_comparison_v1.csv` and `OBSERVED_FINDINGS_v1.txt` preserve the earlier 18-control adverse comparison. Its costs $1511$, $1310$ and $2448$ are retained alongside the expanded 1,536-task replay; they are different evaluations.

These archived controls remain separately identifiable. Their inclusion does not make them part of the seven-entry core workflow or of the 26-program historical stage. The exact stage manifest determines which are executed by the full command.
