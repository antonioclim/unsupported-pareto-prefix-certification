S11.4. SAT Competition 2024: a retained negative-only replay

The official SAT Competition 2024 main-track detailed results provide a second archived-data replay [39]. The declared protocol retains all 14 solver columns and all 400 rows in the supplied instance-hash order. Every unordered solver triple and every designated candidate is used, giving (143)⋅3=1,092\binom{14}{3}\cdot3=1,092 tasks, without filtering by the resulting verdict. The 5,600 score cells include 1,827 exact 10,000 penalties for unsolved instances; every other recorded score was checked to lie in [0,5000][0,5000]. The published aggregate score and solved-count table agrees at its displayed precision with the reconstructed values.

These are normalised PAR-2 penalty and failure objectives, not elapsed CPU time and energy. They satisfy 0≤f≤c≤10\leq f\leq c\leq1. The closed independent triangle is declared as a conservative audit completion contract; it is not asserted to be the exact physical image of benchmark outcomes. Public aggregates and alternative public access are intentionally withheld from the simulated client. A certificate guarantee under that declared contract does not identify the cheapest certificate under a tighter physical domain.

The implemented candidate-census mixture is the retained policy of main Theorem 19, with its exact rational p(G)p(G), rather than the new 87/8087/80 policy.
