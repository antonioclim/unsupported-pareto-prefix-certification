# Component rights for UPPC 1.2.0

Scientific software, retained inputs and evidence preserve their original
component terms. The associated inventory assigns the applicable BSD-3-Clause,
CC BY 4.0 or third-party terms to individual files and nested members.
APEX attribution and DejaVu notices remain part of those components.

The selected Markdown notes, corresponding HTML views and accompanying
bibliographic material derived from the manuscript retain their existing rights.
Public access to these notes confers no additional licence. The complete
manuscript, Essential Supplement and their canonical sources are maintained
with the private submission materials.

In the common release asset, consult `reproduction/LICENSING.md` and
`reproduction/LICENSE_SCOPE.json` for the frozen scientific components. In the
GitHub source tree, consult `LICENSING.md` and the current `LICENSE_SCOPE.json`.
`COMPONENT_SCOPE.json` describes the new public material in both layouts.
