The author adopted BSD-3-Clause for his new code and CC BY 4.0 for his covered
explanatory documentation, tables, metadata and figures on 24 October 2026
(the P35 grant). `LICENSE_SCOPE.json` identifies the covered files and archive
members. The full standard texts are in `LICENSES/`. These are component-specific
terms: a mixed archive does not offer a choice of either licence for every file.

The current article, Supplementary Materials, cover letter, bibliography sources,
author confirmations, private submission audits and portal-form copies remain
outside that public grant. Their existing rights are unchanged. Scientific
checker code, certificate data, result tables, proof notes and figure sources
are identified separately in the scope inventory.

APEX dataset originals and their README retain the upstream CC BY 4.0 terms,
source attribution and dataset DOI https://doi.org/10.3217/3dhx0-2k125.
Secondary extracts and transformations retain that attribution and their change
notices. They are secondary computational analysis, not new physical measurements.
The separate APEX firmware/framework is not bundled or executed; a reference to
that software does not change the terms of the dataset or this collection.

DejaVu fonts, embedded subsets and font-derived glyphs retain the terms in the
bundled `LICENSE_DEJAVU` notices. The author's CC BY 4.0 grant does not replace
those terms. Standard licence texts and original third-party notices retain
their status. External dependencies retain their own terms and are not necessarily
bundled with the material that names them.

Dated pending-licence statements in retained historical evidence describe their
original snapshots. The adopted P35 grant supersedes those statements only for
author-owned components covered by the present scope; it does not relicense
third-party or excluded material. When extracting a historical archive member,
retain the applicable current scope, licence texts and original source notices.

The scope inventory omits hashes of its own root file and of the root package
manifest to avoid a circular dependency. `PACKAGE_MANIFEST_SHA256.json` records
all delivered files except itself, including the scope inventory. Nested and
historical manifests are retained as file contents; an old manifest describes
its original snapshot rather than certifying the current package. The scientific
input manifest, where present, states its separate exclusions explicitly.
